About The Mundo League

Founded in 2023, The Mundo League is a competitive 10-team ESPN Fantasy Football redraft league built on tradition, rivalry, and the pursuit of a championship. The Hall of Fame preserves every champion, every season, and the legacy of The Mundo League for years to come.
